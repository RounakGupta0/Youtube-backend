API LIST 

1. /user

/signup   ,to create a channel
/login    , to login on the app
/subscribe   ,to subscribe a particular channel  (takes channelId in params)
/unsubscribe   ,to unsubscribe a particular channel (takes channelId in params)
/uploadProfilePic   , to upload a profile pic
/uploadCoverPic  , to upload a ProfileCoverPic
/watchLater    , to add to watch Later (takes videoId in params)


2. /video

/uploadVideo   , to upload a Video on the channel
/uploadThumbnail, to update Thumbnail on the channel ( needs videoId on the params)
/like   , a toggle API to like a particular video (needs videoId in params)
/dislike   , a toggle API to dislike a praticula video (needs a videoId in parmas)
/byvideoId   , to get a video by the video Id 
/allVideo, to get all Videos
/byChannelId  , to get videos through channelId
/updateVideo  , to update the information in the video
/deleteById   , to delete a particular video by videoId
/trendingVideo  , to get the tranding videos on the platform (only top 10 videos)

3. /comment

/uploadComment  , to add a comment in the video (takes videoId in params)
/commentsByVideoId  , to get comments of the particular video
/commentBycommentId , to get a comment by commentId (no use for now)
/editComment , to edit a comment 
/like , a toggle API to like a particular comment (takes commentID on params)
/byId , a delete API to delete a particula comment (video owner can also delete)
